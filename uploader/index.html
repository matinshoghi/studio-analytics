Studio Uploader

Studio Uploader is a private, internal tool. It uploads our own finished videos to YouTube channels that we own, with their title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions and translations, and schedules them. It is used only by the owner of those channels and is not offered to the public.

Studio Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Privacy policy

What it accesses. When a channel owner signs in, the tool asks Google for permission to upload videos to that channel and to manage their thumbnails, captions and translated titles and descriptions, and to post the owner's replies to comments on the owner's own videos. It reads the channel's own ID and name to confirm it is uploading to the right channel. It does not read or collect data about other channels or about viewers; the only viewer data it handles is the ID of a comment a reply answers.

What it stores. For each channel: its sign-in token, its ID and its name, kept only on our own computer. For each video it uploads: the video's ID, its privacy setting, the time it was uploaded and the time it is scheduled to go public, kept in our own private project files. For each reply it posts: the reply's text, its ID, the time it was posted and the ID of the comment it answers. Nothing else is stored.

How it is used. Only to upload and schedule our own videos, and to post our replies to comments on them, when the channel owner asks it to. Every upload and every reply starts from the owner's own action, and the owner chooses whether each video is public, private or unlisted. We never sell this data, share it with anyone, or use it for advertising.

Cookies and device storage. The tool runs on our own computers and sets no cookies on anyone else's device.

Removing access and deleting data. Access can be removed at any time at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions; the tool then can no longer reach the channel, and we delete its stored token and channel data within 7 days. You can also ask us to delete it by writing to the address below; we do so within 7 days. Deleting this data does not delete any video from YouTube.

Studio Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
